Ileana's Cards

Card readings in the Romanian tradition, for fun and entertainment

Ileana is a card reader in an app. You ask a question, the cards are laid, and Ileana tells the story she sees in them, in English, Romanian or Turkish. Love Match, The Great Wish, Quick Answer, Nothing to Hide and Fragu's Cross are the readings she offers.

You can sign in with Apple, Google or a code sent to your e-mail. If you wish, you tell Ileana a little about yourself (your name, birthday and how you look) so she can choose your card and remember it.

Readings are for fun and entertainment only. Ileana tells stories; your decisions are always your own.
