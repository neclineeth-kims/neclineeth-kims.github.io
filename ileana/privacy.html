Privacy policy

Ileana's Cards · last updated 24 November 2026

In short

Ileana keeps what you tell her only for your own readings in this app. It is stored encrypted and is never sold, shared or used for anything else. You can change or delete it at any time in the app, under "You".

Signing in

You sign in with Apple, Google or a 6-digit code sent to your e-mail address. Ileana does not store your e-mail address or your Apple or Google account number: she keeps only a one-way fingerprint (a hash) of it, so she can recognise you the next time. From Apple or Google she asks only for your name, not your e-mail address. A small cookie keeps you signed in on your device.

What you tell Ileana ("About you")

Only if you agree, Ileana keeps your full name, whether you are a woman or a man, your birthday, the place and time of your birth (only if you choose to give them), how you look (hair, eyes, skin) and the card she chose for you. This is stored encrypted on the app's server and is used only for your own readings, for example to choose your card, to count the numbers of your name and birthday, or to read a Love Match with you as the first person.

Readings

To write a reading, Ileana sends only what that reading needs (for example the cards, your question if you wrote one, your first name and birthday) to the AI service that writes her words. Your questions and readings are not stored on the server; the journal of your readings stays on your own device. A photo of your cards, if you use that, is only looked at to recognise the cards and is not kept. The app counts how many readings you have had each day, to keep the daily limit.

Services the app uses

  • Hosting of the app: Vercel.
  • The app's small database (accounts, encrypted profiles, daily counters): Upstash.
  • Writing the readings: an AI service reached through OpenRouter.
  • Sending sign-in codes by e-mail: Resend (EU region).
  • Sign-in: Apple and Google, if you choose them.

These services handle the data only to provide their part of the app. There are no advertisements and no tracking.

Deleting your data

In the app, under "You": "Delete what Ileana knows about me" removes your profile at once; "Delete my account" removes your account and everything kept about you. Readings on your device are removed when you delete the app or clear its data.

Children

The app is not meant for children under 16.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
