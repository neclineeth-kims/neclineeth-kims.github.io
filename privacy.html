Privacy policy

Last updated: 6 October 2026

Who uses this app

Nevo Agents is used only by its owner, for their own accounts. It is not offered to the public.

What data is accessed

With the owner's consent, the app reads the owner's own Google data within the permissions the owner grants (for example, read-only access to their Gmail). It does not access any other person's account.

How the data is used

The data is used only to perform tasks the owner asks for, such as finding or summarising the owner's own emails. It is processed on computers controlled by the owner and by the AI model provider the owner has chosen, with data retention for training turned off.

Sharing

The data is not sold, not shared with third parties for their own purposes, and not used for advertising. Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Storage and deletion

Access tokens are stored only on the owner's own computers, readable only by the app's account. The owner can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
